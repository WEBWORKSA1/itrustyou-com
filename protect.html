Questions

Is it really free?
Yes. We may recommend tools — some through affiliate partnerships, always disclosed — but you're never required to buy anything.
Will you ask for my passwords or remote access?
Never. Anyone claiming to be from ITrustYou who asks for those is a scammer.
Can you recover money I lost?
No one can guarantee that, and anyone charging upfront to do so is likely a scammer. We help you contact the banks and agencies that can act.
