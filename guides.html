The 7 signs of any scam

  1. Unexpected contact — you didn't start the conversation.
  2. Urgency or fear — act now, or else.
  3. Secrecy — don't tell your bank, family or anyone.
  4. Unusual payment — gift cards, crypto, wire, P2P apps, cash couriers.
  5. Requests for codes or passwords — always a scam.
  6. Too good to be true — prizes, returns, prices, jobs.
  7. Moving channels — “let's talk on WhatsApp/Telegram.”

Two or more signs? Stop. Verify through a number or website you find yourself. Run it through our checker.

Turn on 2-step verification (2FA)

  1. Start with your email — it's the master key to every other account.
  2. Go to Security settings → 2-Step Verification / Two-factor authentication.
  3. Choose passkeys or an authenticator app over SMS where possible (SMS is vulnerable to SIM swaps).
  4. Save backup codes in your password manager or print them.
  5. Repeat for banking, social media, cloud storage and shopping accounts.

Freeze your credit

A freeze stops lenders from viewing your credit file, so criminals can't open new accounts in your name. You can lift it temporarily when you apply for credit.

  • US: freeze with Equifax, Experian and TransUnion — free by law.
  • Canada: contact Equifax Canada and TransUnion Canada (rules vary by province).
  • UK: use Cifas Protective Registration and check your file with the main agencies.
  • India: monitor your CIBIL report and report misuse to your bank and the cybercrime portal.

Protect older parents (without lecturing)

  • Agree a family safe word for emergency calls.
  • Turn on carrier/phone spam call filtering and silence unknown callers.
  • Put the bank's real number on a sticker on their phone.
  • Set up a trusted contact with their bank where available.
  • Share real examples — our quiz is a friendly way to start.

Shop safely online

  • Check the domain age and reviews — use our checker.
  • Pay by credit card or a protected payment service, never bank transfer or crypto.
  • Look for a real address, phone and returns policy.
  • Brand-new store + massive discounts on popular brands = high risk.

Small-business fraud basics

  • Verify all bank-detail changes by phone on a known number.
  • Require two-person approval for payments above a threshold.
  • Set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC on your domain so criminals can't spoof your email.
  • Register obvious look-alike domains of your brand.
  • Train staff quarterly with real examples.
