Privacy Policy

Effective . Plain language first.

The short version

  • Our checkers run in your browser — what you paste is not sent to us.
  • We only receive what you choose to submit in forms.
  • We never sell your personal information.
  • Ads and analytics use cookies only with your consent where required.

Information we collect

  • Form submissions (reports, safety-plan requests, contact, applications, pledges, contest entries): the details you enter. Forms are delivered via a third-party form-processing service (FormSubmit) to our inbox.
  • Analytics (if enabled): pages visited, device type and approximate region via Google Analytics.
  • Advertising cookies: Google and partners may use cookies to serve and measure ads.
  • Local preferences: theme and cookie choice stored in your browser only.

How we use it

To respond to you, deliver the plan or service you requested, publish anonymised scam warnings (only if you allow it), run contests, improve the site and keep it free through advertising.

Sharing

We share data only with service providers needed to run the site (hosting by GitHub Pages, form delivery, analytics, advertising) or when legally required. With your explicit consent we may pass a scam report to relevant authorities.

Google AdSense & cookies

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits. Opt out at adssettings.google.com. Change your choice here anytime: cookie settings.

Retention

We keep submissions only as long as needed for the purpose, then delete them. Newsletter subscribers can unsubscribe at any time.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR, UK GDPR, PIPEDA, Quebec Law 25, CCPA/CPRA, India DPDP Act), you can request access, correction, deletion or portability, and object to processing. Use our contact form and choose “Privacy request”.

Children

The site is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. Contest entrants under 18 need parental consent.

Changes

We'll update this page and its date when practices change.
