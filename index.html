FAQ

Common questions

Is ITrustYou free?
Yes. All tools, alerts and guides are free. We're funded by advertising, reader support, sponsorships and clearly-labelled affiliate partnerships.
Do you store what I paste into the checkers?
No. The checkers run entirely in your browser. Nothing you paste is transmitted to us. Only forms you choose to submit (like a scam report) are sent.
Can you get my money back?
We can't — and be wary of anyone who promises to for a fee. Our recovery planner shows you the official agencies and banks that can actually help.
Is a high score a guarantee a site is safe?
No. Our checks are heuristics. They catch the most common tricks, but always verify through official channels before paying or sharing information.
Are you affiliated with any government or company?
No. ITrustYou is an independent publication. See our disclosures.
