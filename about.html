“I trust you” should be safe to say.

Scams work by hijacking trust. ITrustYou gives everyone — especially those most targeted — the tools to verify before they trust, and the help to recover when things go wrong.

🎯 Mission

Make scam checking as normal as checking the weather — fast, free and for everyone.

🧭 Independence

Advertisers and partners never influence our warnings, scores or recommendations.

🔒 Privacy

Our checkers run in your browser. We collect only what you choose to submit.

Methodology

Our tools use transparent heuristics drawn from public guidance by consumer-protection agencies and security researchers: impersonation of known brands, look-alike spelling, risky domain endings, hidden redirects, and manipulation language such as urgency, secrecy and unusual payment requests. Scores are estimates — we always link to independent scanners and official sources for confirmation.

Editorial standards

  • Alerts cite official or reputable sources where available.
  • Community reports are reviewed and anonymised before publication.
  • Content is reviewed and dated; corrections are made promptly — tell us if something's wrong.
  • Sponsored content is always labelled.

How we're funded

Display advertising (e.g. Google AdSense), clearly disclosed affiliate partnerships, sponsorships, and reader support. See disclosures.

Not a government agency

ITrustYou is an independent publication. To make an official report, use your national fraud-reporting service — we list them on our report page.
